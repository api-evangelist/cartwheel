# Cartwheel

> Cartwheel is a generative motion platform for games, film & TV, advertising, and robotics — a new way to animate 3D characters from video-to-motion and to pose and render characters in any visual style. Product is delivered as a hosted web studio; there is no public developer/REST API at this time.
